# VigilaLink
VigilaLink turns an Android phone into a security camera, with another phone as the viewer.

It turns an Android phone into a camera, with another phone acting as the viewer. With NetBird running on both devices, you can watch the camera remotely without exposing camera ports publicly or needing a separate VigilaLink cloud service.

Features include:

• Live video and optional camera audio over WebRTC. 
  • Secure QR pairing with device authorization. 
  • Remote front/rear camera switching and flashlight control. 
  • Camera battery status and fullscreen viewing. 
  • Support for local Wi-Fi, mobile hotspots, and VPN connections. 
  • English, Spanish, and Ukrainian translations.
  
It requires Android 12 or newer. I’ve tested it on a Motorola moto g32 and Samsung Galaxy S22+, using both NetBird and WireGuard. 
 
The app is free to use, and I’m currently sharing the signed APK only; the source code is not public. 
 
  Download: [https://github.com/vldmr-d/VigilaLink/releases](https://github.com/vldmr-d/VigilaLink/releases)
 
It’s still a small personal project, so feedback and reports from other devices would be very welcome. I hope someone in the community finds it useful!
